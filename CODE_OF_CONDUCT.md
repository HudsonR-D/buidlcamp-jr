# Code of Conduct

BuidlCamp Junior is a kids coding camp. Be kind. Be honest. Leave the camp better than you found it.

## Expected

- Write for kids and the parents watching over their shoulder
- Keep content age-appropriate
- Never fake a certificate or hide age rules
- Do not add tracking, ads, or surprise accounts
- Assume a real child may read every string you ship

## Not allowed

- Harassment, insults, or creepy behavior
- Sexual content, gore, or hate
- Collecting children’s personal information
- Shipping secrets, telemetry, or “harmless” analytics

## Reports

Open a GitHub issue or email the maintainer listed on the GitHub profile. Maintainers may warn, block, or revert contributions that break this.
